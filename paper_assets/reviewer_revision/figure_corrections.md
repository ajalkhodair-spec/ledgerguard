# Corrected Figure Captions

**Figure 7. Failure-rate sensitivity.** Separate SUCCESS, ROLLBACK, and FAIL
outcome shares for cohorts of 500 software-emulated devices. Points are means
over 20 fixed seeds per configured failure rate; error bars are fixed-seed
10,000-resample bootstrap 95% confidence intervals. Shares use the expected
cohort as denominator. REJECTED and MISSING shares were zero in these runs and
are retained in the underlying five-category summary table. Outcome assignment
is a software experiment, not evidence of physical installation reliability.

**Figure 8. Network sensitivity.** Horizontal box plots and individual points
show all 20 application-shaped local IPFS retrievals per profile, on a
logarithmic time axis. Boxes span Q1 to Q3 with median marks; whiskers follow the
1.5-IQR rule. All observations are retained, including the P3 maximum of
915.736736 seconds. Configured delay and read-rate limits are application-level
controls; the experiment does not reproduce a physical WAN.
